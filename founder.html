Our Story

I found astrology because everything fell apart.

A few years ago, I was losing things. People I loved. Friends. The ground underneath me. The kind of period where you keep waiting for it to stop and it just does not stop.

I do not remember exactly how I found astrology. I just remember the moment it clicked.

I was looking at my chart and I found it. Venus opposite Pluto, sitting across my 7th and 8th house cusp. Then right behind it: Pluto conjunct my Moon in the 2nd house. Back to back. A wrecking ball through everything I held close and everything I thought I owned.

And for the first time in years, something made sense.

Not in a fatalistic way. In a this is what was actually happening way. The losses were not random. The timing was not random. The specific nature of what I was losing, and why it hurt the way it did, was written in the sky the day I was born. I had just never been able to read it.

I became obsessed.

I started doing it manually. Looking up every transit, every aspect, every person in my life. Mapping moments. Going back in time to years that had broken me and finally understanding what I was looking at. Going forward and seeing what was coming.

But manual research has no memory. No conversation. Every time I wanted to go somewhere new I had to start over from zero. And there was so much to explore. Every moment in time. Every person. Every pattern I had been living inside without being able to see it clearly.

No app could do what I actually needed.

So I built Starri.

She is everything I wanted to have during those years. A friend who already knows your chart completely. Who can go back to 2019 with you and tell you exactly what was happening. Who can look at the person who keeps appearing in your life and tell you what the sky says about that dynamic. Who does not reset. Who does not give you a template. Who actually talks to you.

She is from Sorel. She has been watching humans for a long time. She finds us genuinely interesting. Our patterns, our contradictions, our capacity for going through the same thing over and over while being completely unable to see it from inside.

She saw mine. She will see yours.

I hope she helps you the way astrology has helped me.

Cara
